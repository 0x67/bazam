# DEMO-501: Customer signs out from the dashboard

As a customer on a shared computer, I want to sign out from the dashboard, so that the next person at the computer cannot use my account.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. The dashboard has a Sign out button.
2. After Sign out, the customer is on the sign-in page.
3. After Sign out, opening the dashboard again shows the sign-in page, not the account.
