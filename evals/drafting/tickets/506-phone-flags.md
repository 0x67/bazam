# DEMO-506: Country flags in the phone number form

On the Phone number page, the searchable country field lists each country as its flag and its calling code, such as 🇬🇧 +44. Design wants a sign-off that the flags look right in the browser customers use.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. The searchable country list offers the United Kingdom, Indonesia and the United States, each as a flag followed by its calling code.
2. Each flag shows as a flag picture, not as two letters or an empty box. A person has to look at the page to judge this.
