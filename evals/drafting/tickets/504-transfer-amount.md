# DEMO-504: Show the amount and the payee when a transfer is confirmed

Customers who confirm the transfer to Savings with their PIN asked to see what they are confirming, before and after they enter the PIN.

**Acceptance criteria**

1. Before the PIN is entered, the confirmation page reads "Send 25.00 to Savings."
2. Once the PIN is accepted, the page reads "You sent 25.00 to Savings."
